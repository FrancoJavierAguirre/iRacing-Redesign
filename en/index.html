Context

It's December 2021. You're stuck in your room during the pandemic when you read on your phone that some guy named Verstappen has dethroned the 7-time world champion. They say it was an incredibly close race.

You get curious. You binge Drive to Survive and finish it in a week. "There has to be a game that simulates serious racing," you think.

You turn on your PC and start digging. You dust off that old wheel you used to play Need for Speed. Nostalgia hits... and you subscribe to iRacing.

Goals

Business

  • Retain and grow the number of users in the community.
  • Increase the number of purchases on the platform

User

  • Easily find and join an online race they can take part in, based on their level, license and content.
  • Make purchases on the platform easily and intuitively.

The lack of a steady flow of users reduces the availability of competitive races and raises the risk of frustration and churn rate.
