James Garrett's 5 UX Elements

ESEN

Jesse James Garrett

Canadian user experience designer

He is known for his book “The Elements of User Experience” and for co-founding the consulting firm Adaptive Path.

He argues that UX design is built in 5 layers, from the most abstract to the most tangible. Each layer depends on the previous one, which ensures a structured, user-centered process.

Strategy

Research

Go to Scope

Define the business goals and the users’ needs:

Business

  • Retain and grow the number of users in the community.
  • Increase the number of purchases on the platform

User

  • Easily find and join an online race they can take part in, based on their level, license and content.
  • Make purchases on the platform easily and intuitively.

How?

Heuristic evaluations

A review of the interface of the current product to detect usability problems by comparing it against a set of established principles: the heuristics.

Surveys

Collection of quantitative and qualitative data about a user’s experience with a digital product.
