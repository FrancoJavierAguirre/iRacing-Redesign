Context

About iRacing

Go to IntroductionGo to Setting

It’s essentially a car video game. This one in particular is known for being very competitive and realistic, so much so that, to enjoy it, it’s highly recommended to have a steering wheel and pedals connected to the PC.

It requires a monthly subscription, and you race with real car brands and circuits. It simulates real events in format, length and rules.

The game offers competitive races against other players.

The goal of the game depends on each player, but the essence of a racing game is to win races.

Race results will raise or lower your skill rating. This rating is used to match you with similar players.

Another way to progress is through licenses.

Each license unlocks new and more varied races with different formats, cars and tracks.

To move up a license, you need to finish races with few incidents, whether with other players or on your own.

These races always run on a fixed schedule and repeat constantly, every 1 or 2 hours depending on the event.
