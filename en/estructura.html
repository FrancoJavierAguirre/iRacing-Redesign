Conclusions

1Confusing naming

Most users hesitated a lot when making their final choice.

2Ambiguous architecture

The “Teams”, “Spectate” and “More” (results and replays) subcategories, which belong to “Racing”, don’t clearly fit that term.

3Poorly grouped categories

“Home”, “Profile” and “My Content” shouldn’t be inside the “Racing” category, because they contain nothing related to the action of RACING.

Sergio Barrientos

33 years old

Systems Analyst

PC and phone

What he wants to do on the platform

  • Buy a car
  • Join a race

How he’d like to do it

  • A quick and easy purchase
  • Easily find the next race he can take part in.

Task 1

Register for a “D license” “sport cars” race

  1. Start

    The user goes to “Racing”.

  2. Filters by “sport cars” and “D license”.

  3. Opens the race details.

  4. Selects a car as a favorite.

  5. End

    The user joins the race.

Task 2

Buy the car needed to join another race

  1. Start

    Opens the race details

  2. Adds the car to the shopping cart

  3. Opens the shopping cart

  4. Reviews the details before buying

  5. End

    Buys the car
