  • Remove some unnecessary columns from the race table to add the selected favorite car, so clicking “register” doesn’t ask you to pick another car.
  • Replace the “register” button with a menu button that lets you choose another car, or even register for an online practice session or an offline test session.
  • Redesign the information architecture to reduce new users’ hesitation when looking for a race.

  • Use breadcrumb navigation to improve link-based navigation instead of modals.
